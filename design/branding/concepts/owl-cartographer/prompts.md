## Retrato

Use case: logo-brand, exploratory character-symbol concept for GodSense, a tactical game companion about reading the map and anticipating moves. Create ONE NEW close-up owl portrait, not a revision of an esports/tribal mascot. Plain white paper background, no colored rectangle, no badge, no frame, no typography. Crop tightly to the owl's HEAD and a little upper chest; the face fills most of the composition. Slight three-quarter head turn and a subtle curious tilt. An observant adult barn-owl-inspired face with broad softly rounded facial discs, large open amber eyes, a small short beak and a calm intelligent expression. No aggressive V-shaped eyebrows, no long pointed ear tufts, no talons, no wings spread, no full body. The concept is THE CARTOGRAPHER OWL: around the lower facial discs and upper chest, two or three broad feather planes subtly meet like the folds/corridors of a tactical map. This relationship should feel integrated into the owl's anatomy, not a map sticker or a separate prop. Draw natural, rounded, believable feather masses with sparse controlled contour lines; NO slashing negative-space ribbons, NO razor points, NO tribal tattoo curls, NO ornamental feather cuts, NO compass star, NO lightning. Art direction: a thoughtful modern editorial character mark, warm and crafted, neither a cute baby cartoon nor a fierce sports-team animal. Strong dark navy outlines/masses with restrained warm ochre in the irises and a few face/chest accents, paper-white facial feathers. Clear contrast on white and a silhouette that can later be simplified for a small icon. Flat deliberate shapes, no gradient, no grunge, no hatching, no glossy 3D. Aim for a recognizable individual owl personality rather than a generic owl stock logo.

## Refinamiento: mapa en el cuello

Imagen de referencia: portrait.png.

Edit the supplied close-up owl concept for GodSense. Preserve the close framing, the slightly tilted head, the large open amber eyes, the small beak, and the calm intelligent personality. Make the owl feel less like decorative bird illustration and more like a distinctive editorial logo character. Remove most of the individual feather tips, speckles and small gold markings: replace them with a few broad smooth feather masses, with no tribal slashes or pointed ornamental ribbons. Important conceptual change: just beneath the face, turn the upper chest/collar into a clearly recognizable THREE-PANEL FOLDED TACTICAL MAP, integrated as the owl's feather collar rather than a separate object held by the owl. The collar should have a simple clean map fold silhouette and ONE short route linking two solid points, so the map-reader/cartographer relationship is visible, not merely implied. No compass stars, no shield, no wings, no talons, no full body, no lettering, no colored backplate. Use only flat deep navy, warm ochre and the white paper; eliminate beige shading, grey beak shading and gradients. Keep high contrast and clear shapes; the face must remain the dominant subject. Plain white presentation background. This is a close-up CARTOGRAPHER OWL character mark, not an esports mascot or a tribal tattoo.
